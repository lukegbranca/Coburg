About Amanda

Born for this job — dogs know it too

Amanda has built a loyal following in Coburg through one simple thing: a genuine gift with dogs. She's unhurried, warm, and deeply attuned to how each individual dog is feeling — which is exactly why anxious pets walk out happy instead of stressed.

She takes on the dogs other groomers turn away. Mini schnauzers that hate scissors, Frenchies with strong opinions, nervous rescues on their first groom — Amanda handles them all with the patience and care they need.

Regular clients book weeks in advance and clients' dogs look forward to coming back. That says everything about the experience Amanda provides.

— Amanda, Coburg Dog Grooming

What We Offer

Grooming Services

All breeds welcome. Call Amanda for pricing — it varies by breed, size & coat condition.

Full Groom

Bath, blow dry, breed-specific or custom style cut, nail trim and ear clean. All breeds and sizes catered for.

Wash & Blow Dry

Full bath with quality shampoo and conditioner, hand blow dried and brushed through. Includes nail trim.

Nail Trim

Nail clipping for all breeds. Add to any groom or book as a standalone service. Gentle technique for anxious dogs.

Anxious Dog Groom

Specialist handling for nervous, reactive, or difficult-to-groom dogs. Amanda's patience and calm approach makes it possible.
